The WorkBridge Engine Architecture

A deterministic governance lifecycle designed to align incentives, govern scope, and guarantee verified deliverable execution.

Engine A • Pillar 1

Deterministic Scope Baselines & SHA-256 Locking

Functional specifications are managed through a structured versioning protocol. Once both parties ratify the scope document, the platform freezes inputs, writes an immutable SHA-256 cryptographic fingerprint, and restricts revisions to formal bilateral Scope Amendments (v2.0).

  • Semantic requirement versioning (v1.0 baseline locking)
  • Immutable audit logs for all scope modifications
  • Formal amendment workflows with budget and timeline delta tracking
Protocol • Pillar 2

Bilateral Agreement Governance & Multi-Round Redlining

WorkBridge turns ambiguous proposals into actionable digital contracts. Structured terms—including delivery objectives, budget schedules, revision caps, and delivery SLA dates—support inline comments, counter-proposals, and dual review clearance before digital signing.

  • Clause-by-clause comments, counter-terms, and negotiation history
  • Dual-step digital signing with auditable timestamps
  • Instant printable audit trail and export-ready contract view
Engine B • Pillar 3

Weighted Deliverables & Technical Git Verification

Project progression is tied strictly to verified technical deliverables rather than arbitrary completion percentages. Technical leads submit proof metadata—including Git repository links, Pull Request URLs, Commit hashes, and preview staging links.

  • Weighted milestone completion tied directly to approved deliverables
  • Client-facing verification console with direct PR, commit, and demo reviews
  • Bilateral review controls: "Approve & Release Progress" or "Mandate Revision"
Execution • Pillar 4

Sprints, Tasks & Requirements Traceability

Bridge high-level commitments with daily engineering reality. Break milestones into actionable sprint backlogs with task-level statuses, QA verification gates, and automated progress rollups that provide real-time predictability without manual paperwork.

  • Granular sprint backlogs with To-Do, In-Progress, QA, and Done states
  • Requirements-to-deliverables traceability mapping matrix
  • Automated progress rollups from completed tasks to milestone payout release
