The WorkBridge Engine Architecture

A deterministic governance lifecycle designed to align incentives and guarantee verified deliverable execution.

Engine A • Pillar 1

Deterministic Scope Baselines & SHA-256 Locking

Functional specifications are managed through a structured versioning protocol. Once both parties ratify the scope document, the platform freezes inputs, writes an immutable SHA-256 cryptographic fingerprint, and restricts revisions to formal bilateral Scope Amendments (v2.0).

  • Semantic requirement versioning (v1.0 baseline locking)
  • Immutable audit logs for all scope modifications
  • Formal amendment workflows with budget and timeline delta tracking
Protocol • Pillar 2

Bilateral Agreement Governance & Audit Export

WorkBridge turns ambiguous proposals into actionable digital contracts. Structured terms—including delivery objectives, budget schedules, revision caps, and delivery SLA dates—require independent signatures before the workspace unlocks active development.

  • Dual-step digital signing with auditable timestamps
  • Instant printable audit trail and export-ready contract view
  • Rigid invitation guards preventing premature workspace activity
Engine B • Pillar 3

Weighted Deliverables & Simulated Git Verification

Project progression is tied strictly to verified technical deliverables rather than arbitrary completion percentages. Technical leads submit proof metadata—including Git repository links, Pull Request URLs, Commit hashes, and preview staging links.

  • Weighted milestone completion tied to approved deliverables
  • Client-facing verification console with direct PR and staging preview links
  • Bilateral review controls: "Approve & Release Progress" or "Mandate Revision"
Workspace Vault • Pillar 4

Architectural Decision Vault & Resource Management

Centralize all project knowledge within a secure, categorized asset repository. Technical choices made during standup discussions are permanently cataloged in the Architectural Decision Log to eliminate retroactive scope disputes.

  • Structured asset vault for wireframes, API docs, and credential access
  • Permanent architectural decision capture linked to discussions
  • Role-verified standup threads with Client and Lead authentication badges
